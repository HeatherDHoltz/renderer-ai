# Gramophone music

`gramophone.mp3` was supplied by Heather, who confirmed on October 18, 2026 that she
owns it or holds a license to publish it. It ships on the public site. The browser
adds horn filtering and surface hiss during playback.

Other interaction sounds are original procedural Web Audio foley (mechanical
clicks, metal clinks, hinge friction, globe friction, cigar draw and fire crackle),
not recordings of actual antiques. Prop meshes were authored for this project.
No repository-wide license is assigned by this asset credit.
