# Workshop material credits

These photographed materials and HDR panoramas are downloaded from Poly Haven under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original image bytes are retained at 1K; source URLs, upstream MD5 and local SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json). Runtime color tinting, UV scaling and strength adjustments are project-created.

Velour velvet supplies actual photographed nap, normal, roughness and occlusion maps, recolored green and burgundy in the shader. Rosewood supplies casework, bench tools and support. The plaster and scanned-table maps are preserved from the previous rustic room; the current atelier uses Blender-modeled and baked surfaces instead. Artist Workshop initializes room illumination; Forest Slope supplies the outdoor window panorama. A cached live cube capture of the room drives gem/metal reflections after initialization; faceted gem rays also intersect fixture-free finite examination apertures. The fixed fabric-shaded lamps belong to the Blender-baked room.

The painting above the fireplace is William Trost Richards’ *Sundown at Centre Harbor, New Hampshire* (1874), from [the Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 11909](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11909). The Met marks the image public domain through Open Access. [Painting source and room asset hashes](../atelier-provenance.json).

## Historical diamond mesh

[Jewel by Zsky](https://poly.pizza/m/velVo80s1D), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). `../diamond.obj` is a byte-exact copy of the repository’s adapted `assets/gems/zsky-jewel-01.obj`, also used in the Rust gem panel. Runtime scale, flat normals, optical material and ring mount are project adaptations. The 21-plane cut is the source artist’s design, not a certified 57-facet brilliant. [Full upstream conversion/adaptation notices](https://github.com/HeatherDHoltz/renderer-ai/blob/main/assets/gems/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Cabochon and step-cut geometry are project-created. The live faceted collection now uses project-created brilliant, step, oval and cushion solids. The earlier meshes remain as offline references. See [the color and geometry study](https://github.com/HeatherDHoltz/renderer-ai/blob/main/docs/GEM-COLOR.md). No project-wide source license is assigned.
