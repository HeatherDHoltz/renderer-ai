Historical diamond

Jewel by Zsky, CC BY 3.0. Same adapted OBJ as the Rust diamond; runtime normalization, flat shading, materials and mounting are project adaptations. The source design has 21 optical planes, not a certified 57-facet brilliant. Full conversion and adaptation notices.

Cabochon and emerald geometry are project-created; the live collection now uses new brilliant, step, oval and cushion solids. The prior meshes remain offline references. No repository-wide license has been selected.

The contemporary room is now project-modeled in Blender/Cycles. The photographed velvet is recolored burgundy and green on matching lids and trays; older plaster and table maps are preserved but unused. Current color, geometry and room study.

The mantel painting is William Trost Richards’ Sundown at Centre Harbor, New Hampshire (1874), courtesy of The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 11909. Public domain under Met Open Access. Painting and Blender room provenance.
